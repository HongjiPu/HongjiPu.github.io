<div style="max-width:62ch;margin:0 0 48px;">
  <p>My research focuses on <strong>self-evolving language agents</strong>, <strong>graph world models</strong>, and <strong>trustworthy LLM systems</strong>. I study how agents can learn from experience, external knowledge, and verified feedback to improve their memory, reusable skills, and decision-making — and how world models can support algorithm design on complex networks.</p>
  <p>Across these directions I build methods and evaluations for systems that continually adapt to new tasks and environments, with an emphasis on the reliability of both their behaviour and their claimed improvements.</p>
